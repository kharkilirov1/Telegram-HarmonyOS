# TASKS/ARCHIVE — history, not instructions

Read-only history. **Nothing here is executed, current, or maintained.** Active documents live one
level up: `TASKS/TODO.md` (queue), `TASKS/AGENT_EXECUTION_PLAN.md` (route), `TASKS/PRODUCTION_PARITY_PLAN.md`
(exit criteria), `TASKS/LESSONS.md` (numbered lessons), plus the three open gates
(`CHAT_ACCESS_PLAN.md`, `CHAT_ROUTE_OWNERSHIP_PLAN.md`, `TDLIB_RECOVERY_PLAN.md`).

Two conventions apply to everything below:

- Links to build outputs (`entry/build/**/*.hap`), offline evidence JSON, and absolute
  `C:/Users/...` / `C:/Refs/...` paths may be dead by design — they were valid on their date.
- Do not "update" an archived report to match today's code. If it is wrong, write a new dated report
  in `docs/reviews/` and let it supersede.
