# Logo research — archive

Working artefacts from the app-icon exploration (2026-07…08). Kept for provenance only.

**None of these is the chosen icon.** The four approved-candidate SVGs live one level up in
`design/logo/` (`logo-appicon-light.svg`, `logo-appicon-dark.svg`, `logo-orbit.svg`,
`logo-wordmark.svg`); the final choice is still pending a user decision.

Contents:

- `icon-research-harmonyos.md` — HarmonyOS icon-shape and safe-area research.
- `icon_v3_*` — third iteration: layered foreground/background sources, composites, HTML preview.
- `app_icon_harmony_*` — earlier previews and cropped composites.
- `icon_capture.html`, `tab_icons_preview.html` — capture/preview pages used to render the PNGs.

Moved here from `docs/` on 2026-10-18: nothing referenced these paths, and the icons were
sitting next to the review reports where they read as current deliverables.
