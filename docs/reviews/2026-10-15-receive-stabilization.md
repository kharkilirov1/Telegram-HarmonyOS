## Behavior

DTO/auth/adapter/dispatcher corrections remove the reviewed raw payload/error logging and preserve typed error provenance and silent TDLib406 handling. Duplicate auth handling is removed. Privacy tests exercise the actual source and captured sinks; this does not assert repository-wide or packaged-TDLib log redaction.

The native receive ledger charges waiting, admitted and actively parsed batches until exact epoch/batch consumed ACK. The window is four batches/16MiB of UTF8 payload; a batch is at most 50 responses and 8MiB including framing, with a 256KiB target. One separately bounded builder and one lookahead response add at most roughly16MiB. These are logical bridge payload limits, not total RSS, VM/TDLib/store memory or asynchronous downstream hydration bounds.

Pre-JS conversion retains the front batch for up to three paced attempts only without a pending exception. Possible JS execution is never replayed. Ambiguous notification failure is terminal; only known queue-full retries. The one pending wake lease is distinct from physical queued-plus-executing callbacks, which may reach two. Numeric status/watchdog faults reject dispatched requests with unknown outcome, retain a live client owner and never resend or fabricate Closed.

Stop cancels admission and waits for off-owner producer join, actual TSFN finalization and control/submission lease retirement. An already admitted NAPI call may enter after stop is requested while its lease protects ownership; new admissions are closed. Cleanup/control failures retain the barrier instead of claiming success. Actual Closed is required before ordinary client retirement; native stop alone is not proof of client release.
