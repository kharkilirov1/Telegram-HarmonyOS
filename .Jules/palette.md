## 2024-10-18 - TgChatTopBar interactive touch targets and accessibility
**Learning:** In complex navigation layouts like `TgChatTopBar`, interactive icons (`ic_search`, `ic_close`) were placed bare without layout wrappers, lacking both adequate touch target sizing (44vp standard) and proper screen reader descriptions. Applying click handlers to bare icons creates tiny touch targets and prevents the standard `.clickEffect()` from displaying the appropriate circular ripple. Furthermore, complex inner containers like the title block need dynamic `.accessibilityDescription` based on their state to ensure screen readers announce status accurately (e.g. online, typing, etc) rather than fragmenting the text.
**Action:** When making custom container components interactive and accessible, wrap standalone interactive icons in appropriately sized layout containers (like `Row`), move margins and `onClick` handlers to the wrapper, apply `.clickEffect({ level: ClickEffectLevel.LIGHT })` to the wrapper, and explicitly apply `.accessibilityGroup(true)` and `.accessibilityDescription()` to all interactive parents to prevent fragmented screen reader announcements and ensure compliant touch targets.
