## 2026-04-30 - Adding accessibility to icon-only buttons\n**Learning:** ArkUI custom components like `TgIcon` require wrapping in a standard container (like `Row`) to properly apply accessibility attributes and handle interaction events.\n**Action:** When adding accessibility to interactive custom icons, always wrap them in a container, move `onClick` and margins to the container, and apply `.accessibilityGroup(true)` and `.accessibilityDescription()` to the wrapper.
